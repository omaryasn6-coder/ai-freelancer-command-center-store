Frequently asked questions

How do I receive my purchase?

Every edition is delivered instantly as a digital download (ZIP) through Gumroad. No account with this site is required.

Is this a native Notion template I can duplicate with one click?

No - it's a Notion-portable AI freelancer operating system delivered as CSV imports, database schemas, example workflows, and setup instructions. You import the CSVs into your own Notion workspace following the guide, typically 10-40 minutes.

Do I need a paid Notion plan for the Command Center?

No. Notion's free plan supports everything in both the Lite and Pro editions.

Can I use the Command Center outside Notion?

Yes - it's delivered as CSV files that import into Airtable, Coda, or any spreadsheet tool, plus written schema docs describing the structure in tool-agnostic terms.

Will the Command Center get me clients?

No. It's an organizational system for work you already have or are pursuing - it doesn't generate leads or guarantee income.

Are the example client names and figures real?

No. Every example row across every database is fictional demonstration data, clearly marked [DEMO]. A headers-only blank CSV is included for every database if you'd rather start empty.

What's your refund policy?

Refunds follow Gumroad's standard digital-goods policy, published at checkout.

Can I resell or share these files?

No. Every product is licensed for your own personal or single-business use - see the Terms page and each product's included license file.

Is this content AI-generated?

Structure, guides, and design were created with AI-assisted tools and human-reviewed before listing. This is disclosed on every product listing, not just here.
